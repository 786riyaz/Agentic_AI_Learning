MS Dhoni is a former Indian cricket team captain. He is known for his calm leadership and finishing ability. Dhoni is widely regarded as one of the best finishers in the history of cricket.

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He is known for his aggressive batting style and consistency across formats. Kohli has scored numerous centuries in international cricket.

Rohit Sharma is an Indian cricketer and the captain of the Indian team in limited overs formats. He is known for his elegant batting and record-breaking double centuries in One Day Internationals.

Sachin Tendulkar is regarded as one of the greatest cricketers of all time. He has scored 100 international centuries and is often called the "God of Cricket".

MS Dhoni led India to multiple ICC trophies including the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy.

Virat Kohli has been one of the fastest players to reach 8000, 9000, and 10000 runs in ODIs.

Rohit Sharma holds the record for the highest individual score in ODIs (264).

Sachin Tendulkar played for over two decades and retired as the highest run-scorer in international cricket.
