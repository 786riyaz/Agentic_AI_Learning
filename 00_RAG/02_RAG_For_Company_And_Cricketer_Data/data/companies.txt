OpenAI is an artificial intelligence company focused on developing advanced AI models and research. The CEO of OpenAI is Sam Altman.

Google is a multinational technology company specializing in search, cloud computing, and AI. The CEO of Google is Sundar Pichai.

Microsoft is a global technology company known for software, cloud services, and enterprise solutions. The CEO of Microsoft is Satya Nadella.

OpenAI develops models like GPT for natural language processing and generative AI applications.

Google owns products like Gmail, YouTube, and Google Cloud Platform.

Microsoft develops products like Windows, Azure, and Microsoft Office.

Sam Altman has been instrumental in scaling OpenAI and advancing AI safety initiatives.

Sundar Pichai has led Google through significant growth in AI and cloud computing.

Satya Nadella transformed Microsoft into a cloud-first company with Azure as a core offering.
